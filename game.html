Free wave jackpots

Most cleared waves have no jackpot bonus (70%). Lucky waves award 25 credits (20%), 100 credits (8%), or a 500-credit JACKPOT (2%). Normal wave-clear credits are always awarded. Every 10th wave guarantees 500 credits instead. No credits are spent. Rewards save with your progress.

Progress saves automatically in this browser. Leaving during a wave restores its starting checkpoint.

Emergency support: once per run, an attacker reaching the final 90 pixels of the road grants 1,000 credits, drops double-ammo crates for each deployed defender type, and pauses the wave so you can strengthen your defense.

Ammo: wait for parachute crates to land, then click them to store matching ammo. Click a placed defender to reload only that defender from matching storage and open its upgrade panel. More defenders bring more ammo: enemy drops start at 6%, adding 1 percentage point per extra defender (up to 30%). Supply deliveries also speed up with your crew, from every 40–60 seconds to as often as every 10–15 seconds. Crates remain between waves. New defenders start fully loaded.
